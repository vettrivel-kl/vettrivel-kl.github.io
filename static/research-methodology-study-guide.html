02. Design of Experiments (DoE) & Taguchi Optimization

Linear Models: CRD vs. RBD

Completely Randomized Design (CRD): $Y_{ij} = \mu + \tau_i + \epsilon_{ij}, \quad df_{\text{Error}} = N - k$ (Homogeneous units)

Randomized Block Design (RBD): $Y_{ij} = \mu + \tau_i + \beta_j + \epsilon_{ij}, \quad df_{\text{Error}} = (k - 1)(b - 1)$ (Heterogeneous units grouped into $b$ blocks)

Taguchi Robust Design & S/N Ratios

• $L_9(3^4)$ Array: Optimizes 3 factors at 3 levels in just 9 runs (instead of $3^3 = 27$ runs).

$$\text{Larger-the-Better (Yield): } \eta = -10 \log_{10}\left( \frac{1}{n} \sum \frac{1}{y_i^2} \right), \quad \text{Smaller-the-Better (Defects): } \eta = -10 \log_{10}\left( \frac{1}{n} \sum y_i^2 \right)$$

Rule: Higher S/N ratio always indicates optimal robust performance.

03. Research Ethics, Misconduct & Predatory Journals

The FFP Triad & Misconduct Case

• Fabrication: Making up raw data out of whole cloth.

• Falsification: Altering genuine experimental readings or cherry-picking data points.

• Plagiarism: Appropriating ideas, code, or text without attribution.

Dr. Rajesh Kumar Case: Fabricated water purification data $\to$ paper retracted, fellowship cancelled, 3-5 year funding ban, university NAAC points docked. Prevention: mandatory raw data archiving.

Salami Slicing & Jeffrey Beall's Criteria

• Salami Slicing: Chapping a study into Least Publishable Units (LPUs) $\to$ distorts meta-analyses.

• Predatory Journals: Guaranteed rapid acceptance (< 72h), fake metrics (Universal Impact Factor), hidden APCs, lack of digital preservation.

• Singapore Statement (2010): Honesty, Accountability, Professional Courtesy, Good Stewardship.

04. Statistical Analysis & Hypothesis Testing (r & χ²)

Karl Pearson's Correlation (Father/Son Heights)

$$r = \frac{\sum (X - \bar{X})(Y - \bar{Y})}{\sqrt{\sum (X - \bar{X})^2 \sum (Y - \bar{Y})^2}} = \frac{24}{\sqrt{36 \times 44}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{11}} \approx \mathbf{+0.6030}$$

Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit (Aircraft Accidents)

$$\chi^2 = \sum_{i=1}^k \frac{(O_i - E_i)^2}{E_i} = \frac{30}{14} = \frac{15}{7} \approx \mathbf{2.1429}$$

Degrees of freedom: $df = 6 - 1 = 5$. Since $\chi^2_{\text{calc}} = 2.1429 < \chi^2_{0.05, 5} = 11.070 \implies$ Fail to reject $H_0$ (Uniform distribution confirmed).

05. Scientific Dissemination, Citations & Thesis Blueprint

Citation Style Conversion Rules

APA 7th: `Gupta, R. K., & Mehta, S. (2021). Solar PV efficiency improvements in India. Journal of Renewable Energy Research, 12(3), 145–158.` (All inverted, year after author in parens, title sentence case unquoted)

IEEE: `R. K. Gupta and S. Mehta, "Solar PV efficiency improvements in India," J. Renew. Energy Res., vol. 12, no. 3, pp. 145–158, 2021.` (No inversion, year at very end, title in quotes)

Chicago: `Gupta, R. K., and S. Mehta. 2021. "Solar PV Efficiency Improvements in India." Journal of Renewable Energy Research 12 (3): 145–158.` (Only first author inverted, year after authors without parens)

Postgraduate Thesis Chapters 1 to 6

Preliminary Matter $\to$ Ch 1: Intro $\to$ Ch 2: Lit Review (Gap) $\to$ Ch 3: Methodology $\to$ Ch 4: Implementation $\to$ Ch 5: Results & Discussion $\to$ Ch 6: Conclusion $\to$ Back Matter (References, Appendices).

02. Informed Heuristic Search (A* & AO*)

A* Evaluation Function & Admissibility

$$f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$$

Admissibility: $0 \le h(n) \le h^*(n)$ (Never overestimates true minimal cost). Guarantees optimal path.

Consistency (Monotonicity): $h(n) \le c(n, a, n') + h(n') \implies f(n) \le f(n')$. Guarantees $f(n)$ is non-decreasing along paths.

8-Puzzle Manhattan Distance Heuristic

$$h(n) = \sum_{i=1}^8 \left( |x_i - x_i^{\text{goal}}| + |y_i - y_i^{\text{goal}}| \right)$$

Benchmark: May 2025 Initial State has $h(S_0) = 5$, $g(S_0) = 0 \implies f(n) = 5$ constant at all 5 optimal moves.

AO* And-Or Branch Cost Propagation

$$\text{OR Branch: } \operatorname{Cost}(n) = \min_i [c(n, n_i) + h(n_i)], \qquad \text{AND Branch: } \operatorname{Cost}(n) = \sum_j [c(n, n_j) + h(n_j)]$$

03. Adversarial Search & Constraint Satisfaction

Minimax & Alpha-Beta Pruning Invariant

$$\alpha \ge \beta \implies \text{PRUNE remaining sibling branches!}$$

$\alpha = \max(\alpha, v)$ updated at MAX nodes ($\alpha = -\infty$). $\beta = \min(\beta, v)$ updated at MIN nodes ($\beta = +\infty$).

SEND + MORE = MONEY Cryptarithmetic Solution

$$\begin{matrix} & 9 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\ + & 1 & 0 & 8 & 5 \\ \hline 1 & 0 & 6 & 5 & 2 \end{matrix}$$ $$M = 1, \ O = 0, \ S = 9, \ E = 5, \ N = 6, \ R = 8, \ D = 7, \ Y = 2$$ $$\mathbf{N + E + E + D = 6 + 5 + 5 + 7 = 23}$$

04. Uninformed Search Traversal & Complexities

Search Algorithm Data Structure Complete? Optimal? Time Space
Breadth-First (BFS) FIFO Queue Yes Yes (unit cost) $O(b^d)$ $O(b^d)$ (Exponential)
Depth-First (DFS) LIFO Stack No (infinite trees) No $O(b^m)$ $O(bm)$ (Linear)
Depth-Limited (DLS) LIFO Stack (Limit $L$) Yes (if $d \le L$) No $O(b^L)$ $O(bL)$ (Linear)
Iterative Deepening (IDS) LIFO Stack (Repeated) Yes Yes (unit cost) $O(b^d)$ $O(bd)$ (Optimal + Linear)

Hill Climbing 3 Hazards

1. Local Maxima: Peak lower than global optimum $\to$ Remedy: Random Restart.

2. Plateau / Shoulder: Flat zero gradient $\to$ Remedy: Limited sideways moves.

3. Ridge: Steep sides, gentle spine $\to$ Remedy: Simulated Annealing ($P = e^{\Delta E / T}$).

05. Knowledge Representation & Bayesian Networks

The Alarm Bayesian Network Solution

$$P(A \mid J, M, \neg E, \neg B) = \frac{P(A \mid \neg B, \neg E)P(J \mid A)P(M \mid A)}{\sum_{a} P(a \mid \neg B, \neg E)P(J \mid a)P(M \mid a)}$$ $$= \frac{0.001 \times 0.90 \times 0.70}{(0.001 \times 0.90 \times 0.70) + (0.999 \times 0.05 \times 0.01)} = \frac{0.000630}{0.0011295} = \frac{140}{251} \approx \mathbf{55.78\%}$$

Minsky's Frames & Chaining

Frames Structure: Slots (attributes), Facets (defaults/ranges), and Procedural Demons (`if-needed`, `if-added`, `if-removed`).

Forward Chaining: Data-driven ($\text{IF} \to \text{THEN}$) starting from facts (CLIPS, Rete).

Backward Chaining: Goal-driven ($\text{THEN} \to \text{IF}$) starting from hypothesis (Prolog, MYCIN).

06. Python Core, Pandas & Visualization

Mutability & Memory Proof

Strings are Immutable: Modification raises `TypeError`. Creating/concatenating alters `id(obj)`.

Lists are Mutable: In-place `append()` retains identical `id(obj)`.

Essential Pandas Commands

• Missing Data: `df.isnull().sum()`, `df.fillna(df['col'].mean(), inplace=True)`, `df.dropna(subset=['col'])`

• GroupBy: `df.groupby('Store')['Sales'].agg(['sum', 'mean'])`

• Filtering: `df[(df['Category'] == 'Electronics') & (df['Total_Sales'] > 10000)]`

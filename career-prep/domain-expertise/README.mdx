# Domain Expertise & Product Architecture

[Back to Master Dashboard](../index.mdx)

:::info

**Domain Mastery in Senior & Staff Engineering**

At senior and staff levels, technical decisions are evaluated not in a vacuum, but by their measurable impact on product metrics, user experience, reliability, and business revenue. Use this workspace to document domain architecture, key business KPIs, and critical user journeys.

:::

## Strategic Framework

When discussing domain expertise and system architecture in technical evaluations, use this 4-pillar structured breakdown:

1. **Business Context & KPIs:**
   - What core business problem does the system solve?
   - What are the primary business metrics (e.g., Conversion Rate, GMV, Customer Acquisition Cost, Daily Active Users)?
   - What are the primary technical metrics (e.g., P99 Latency, Availability SLA, Error Budget, Order Processing Throughput)?

2. **Core User Personas & Critical User Journeys (CUJs):**
   - Identify the primary actors interacting with the platform.
   - Map out the end-to-end dataflow for the highest-value transaction or workflow.
   - Highlight potential friction points, failure modes, and recovery mechanisms.

3. **Architectural Trade-offs & Business Alignment:**
   - How do database selection and caching strategies directly influence transaction success rates?
   - What trade-offs were made between immediate consistency and high availability (e.g., in checkout vs. analytics feeds)?
   - How does infrastructure cost scale relative to tenant/user growth?

4. **Executive & Cross-Functional Communication:**
   - Synthesize complex architectural initiatives into concise 3-minute executive summaries.
   - Quantify engineering ROI (e.g., "Refactoring the checkout service reduced P99 latency by 45%, driving a 1.2% lift in checkout conversion").

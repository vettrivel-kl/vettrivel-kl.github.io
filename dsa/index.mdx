## Algorithmic Complexity Reference

:::note

**Asymptotic Notations**

- **$O(g(n))$ (Upper Bound):** $f(n) \le c \cdot g(n)$ for all $n \ge n_0$.
- **$\Omega(g(n))$ (Lower Bound):** $f(n) \ge c \cdot g(n)$ for all $n \ge n_0$.
- **$\Theta(g(n))$ (Tight Bound):** $c_1 \cdot g(n) \le f(n) \le c_2 \cdot g(n)$ for all $n \ge n_0$.

:::

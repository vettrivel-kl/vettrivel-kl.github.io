# Tier 2: Scale & Concurrency (Sprints 13-24)

[Back to Master Dashboard](../index.mdx)

:::info

**Tier 2 Strategic Goal**

Move from single-component fundamentals to production-scale distributed complexity:
- **DSA:** Graphs, Topological Sort, Union-Find, Intervals, and non-linear data structures.
- **Backend HLD:** Distributed transactions, Saga orchestration, Outbox pattern, and geospatial indexing.
- **Frontend HLD:** Real-time collaboration, CRDTs, client outbox queues, and performance profiling.
- **LLD:** Multithreaded systems in Java, in-memory event buses, and complex state machines.
- **Leadership:** Handling deep architectural disagreements, mentoring leads, and driving cross-team adoption.

:::

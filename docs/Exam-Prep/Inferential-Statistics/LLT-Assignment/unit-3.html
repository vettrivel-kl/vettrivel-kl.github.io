Question 1​

info

Question Statement

In a recent survey conducted to evaluate customer satisfaction at a local retail store, 300 customers were sampled. Among the respondents, 180 reported that they were satisfied with their shopping experience.Calculate the 90%, 95%, 99% confidence interval for the proportion of all customers (assuming a total of 1000 customers) who are satisfied with their shopping experience at the retail store.

Solution 1​

Step 1: Identify Parameters​
  • Total Population Size (NNN): 100010001000
  • Sample Size (nnn): 300300300
  • Number of Satisfied Customers (xxx): 180180180
  • Sample Proportion (p^\hat{p}p^​): p^=xn=180300=0.60 (or 60%)\hat{p} = \frac{x}{n} = \frac{180}{300} = \mathbf{0.60}\text{ (or } 60\%\text{)}p^​=nx​=300180​=0.60 (or 60%)
  • Complement Proportion (q^\hat{q}q^​): q^=1−p^=1−0.60=0.40 (or 40%)\hat{q} = 1 - \hat{p} = 1 - 0.60 = \mathbf{0.40}\text{ (or } 40\%\text{)}q^​=1−p^​=1−0.60=0.40 (or 40%)
Step 2: Standard Error & Finite Population Correction (FPC)​

  • Standard Error (SESESE): SE=p^q^n=0.60×0.40300=0.24300=0.028284SE = \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}\hat{q}}{n}} = \sqrt{\frac{0.60 \times 0.40}{300}} = \sqrt{\frac{0.24}{300}} = \mathbf{0.028284}SE=np^​q^​​​=3000.60×0.40​​=3000.24​​=0.028284

  • Finite Population Correction (FPC Factor): Because the sampling fraction nN=3001000=30%>5%\frac{n}{N} = \frac{300}{1000} = 30\% > 5\%Nn​=1000300​=30%>5%, the FPC must be applied: FPC Factor=N−nN−1=1000−3001000−1=700999≈0.837079\text{FPC Factor} = \sqrt{\frac{N - n}{N - 1}} = \sqrt{\frac{1000 - 300}{1000 - 1}} = \sqrt{\frac{700}{999}} \approx \mathbf{0.837079}FPC Factor=N−1N−n​​=1000−11000−300​​=999700​​≈0.837079

  • Adjusted Standard Error (SEadjSE_{\text{adj}}SEadj​): SEadj=SE×FPC=0.028284×0.837079=0.023676SE_{\text{adj}} = SE \times \text{FPC} = 0.028284 \times 0.837079 = \mathbf{0.023676}SEadj​=SE×FPC=0.028284×0.837079=0.023676

Step 3: Confidence Interval Formula​

CI=p^±(Z×SEadj)\text{CI} = \hat{p} \pm (Z \times SE_{\text{adj}})CI=p^​±(Z×SEadj​)

Subpart Evaluations​
(i) 90% Confidence Interval​
  • Critical Value (ZZZ): 1.6451.6451.645
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 1.645×0.023676=0.0389471.645 \times 0.023676 = \mathbf{0.038947}1.645×0.023676=0.038947
  • Lower Limit: 0.60−0.038947=0.5610530.60 - 0.038947 = \mathbf{0.561053}0.60−0.038947=0.561053
  • Upper Limit: 0.60+0.038947=0.6389470.60 + 0.038947 = \mathbf{0.638947}0.60+0.038947=0.638947
  • Interval: [56.11%, 63.89%]\mathbf{[56.11\%, \, 63.89\%]}[56.11%,63.89%] (or [0.5611, 0.6389][0.5611, \, 0.6389][0.5611,0.6389])
(ii) 95% Confidence Interval​
  • Critical Value (ZZZ): 1.9601.9601.960
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 1.960×0.023676=0.0464051.960 \times 0.023676 = \mathbf{0.046405}1.960×0.023676=0.046405
  • Lower Limit: 0.60−0.046405=0.5535950.60 - 0.046405 = \mathbf{0.553595}0.60−0.046405=0.553595
  • Upper Limit: 0.60+0.046405=0.6464050.60 + 0.046405 = \mathbf{0.646405}0.60+0.046405=0.646405
  • Interval: [55.36%, 64.64%]\mathbf{[55.36\%, \, 64.64\%]}[55.36%,64.64%] (or [0.5536, 0.6464][0.5536, \, 0.6464][0.5536,0.6464])
(iii) 99% Confidence Interval​
  • Critical Value (ZZZ): 2.5762.5762.576
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 2.576×0.023676=0.0609892.576 \times 0.023676 = \mathbf{0.060989}2.576×0.023676=0.060989
  • Lower Limit: 0.60−0.060989=0.5390110.60 - 0.060989 = \mathbf{0.539011}0.60−0.060989=0.539011
  • Upper Limit: 0.60+0.060989=0.6609890.60 + 0.060989 = \mathbf{0.660989}0.60+0.060989=0.660989
  • Interval: [53.90%, 66.10%]\mathbf{[53.90\%, \, 66.10\%]}[53.90%,66.10%] (or [0.5390, 0.6610][0.5390, \, 0.6610][0.5390,0.6610])

Question 2​

info

Question Statement

A company claims that the average time employees spend on training is 20 hours per month. A random sample of 36 employees is taken, and it is found that the average time spent on training is 22 hours with a population standard deviation of 6 hours. At a 90%, 95%,99% confidence level, can we conclude that the average training time for employees is significantly different from 20 hours? Assume data is normally distributed and calculate the confidence interval for the mean training time.

Solution 2​

Step 1: Identify Parameters​
  • Claimed Population Mean (μ0\mu_0μ0​): 20 hours20\text{ hours}20 hours
  • Sample Mean (xˉ\bar{x}xˉ): 22 hours22\text{ hours}22 hours
  • Population Standard Deviation (σ\sigmaσ): 6 hours6\text{ hours}6 hours
  • Sample Size (nnn): 36 employees36\text{ employees}36 employees
Step 2: Calculate Standard Error (SESESE)​

SE=σn=636=66=1.0 hourSE = \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{36}} = \frac{6}{6} = \mathbf{1.0\text{ hour}}SE=n​σ​=36​6​=66​=1.0 hour

Step 3: Confidence Interval Formula​

CI=xˉ±(Z×SE)\text{CI} = \bar{x} \pm (Z \times SE)CI=xˉ±(Z×SE)

Subpart Evaluations across Confidence Levels​
(i) 90% Confidence Level​
  • Critical Value (ZZZ): 1.6451.6451.645
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 1.645×1.0=1.6451.645 \times 1.0 = \mathbf{1.645}1.645×1.0=1.645
  • Lower Limit: 22−1.645=20.35522 - 1.645 = \mathbf{20.355}22−1.645=20.355
  • Upper Limit: 22+1.645=23.64522 + 1.645 = \mathbf{23.645}22+1.645=23.645
  • Confidence Interval: [20.36, 23.64] hours\mathbf{[20.36, \, 23.64]\text{ hours}}[20.36,23.64] hours (or [20.355, 23.645][20.355, \, 23.645][20.355,23.645])
  • Decision: The hypothesized value of 20h20\text{h}20h sits outside the interval (20∉[20.36,23.64]20 \notin [20.36, 23.64]20∈/[20.36,23.64])   ⟹  \implies⟹ Reject H0H_0H0​ (significantly different).
(ii) 95% Confidence Level​
  • Critical Value (ZZZ): 1.9601.9601.960
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 1.960×1.0=1.9601.960 \times 1.0 = \mathbf{1.960}1.960×1.0=1.960
  • Lower Limit: 22−1.960=20.04022 - 1.960 = \mathbf{20.040}22−1.960=20.040
  • Upper Limit: 22+1.960=23.96022 + 1.960 = \mathbf{23.960}22+1.960=23.960
  • Confidence Interval: [20.040, 23.960] hours\mathbf{[20.040, \, 23.960]\text{ hours}}[20.040,23.960] hours
  • Decision: The hypothesized value of 20h20\text{h}20h still falls outside this interval (20∉[20.040,23.960]20 \notin [20.040, 23.960]20∈/[20.040,23.960])   ⟹  \implies⟹ Reject H0H_0H0​ (significantly different).
(iii) 99% Confidence Level​
  • Critical Value (ZZZ): 2.5762.5762.576
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 2.576×1.0=2.5762.576 \times 1.0 = \mathbf{2.576}2.576×1.0=2.576
  • Lower Limit: 22−2.576=19.42422 - 2.576 = \mathbf{19.424}22−2.576=19.424
  • Upper Limit: 22+2.576=24.57622 + 2.576 = \mathbf{24.576}22+2.576=24.576
  • Confidence Interval: [19.424, 24.576] hours\mathbf{[19.424, \, 24.576]\text{ hours}}[19.424,24.576] hours
  • Decision: The hypothesized value of 20h20\text{h}20h falls inside this wider interval (20∈[19.424,24.576]20 \in [19.424, 24.576]20∈[19.424,24.576])   ⟹  \implies⟹ Fail to Reject H0H_0H0​ (not significantly different).

Question 3​

Question Statement

A researcher wants to assess the effect of a new study method on student performance. He collects test scores from a sample of 15 students who used the new method. The mean score of the sample is 78, and the standard deviation is 10. At a 90%,95% and 99% confidence level, what is the confidence interval for the average test score of all students using the new study method? Perform the calculation Confident Intervals.

Solution 3​

Step 1: Identify Parameters & Degrees of Freedom​
  • Sample Mean (xˉ\bar{x}xˉ): 787878
  • Sample Standard Deviation (sss): 101010
  • Sample Size (nnn): 151515
  • Degrees of Freedom (dfdfdf): df=n−1=15−1=14df = n - 1 = 15 - 1 = \mathbf{14}df=n−1=15−1=14
Step 2: Calculate Standard Error (SESESE)​

SE=sn=1015=103.8730≈2.5820SE = \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{10}{\sqrt{15}} = \frac{10}{3.8730} \approx \mathbf{2.5820}SE=n​s​=15​10​=3.873010​≈2.5820

Step 3: Confidence Interval Formula​

CI=xˉ±(tc×SE)\text{CI} = \bar{x} \pm (t_c \times SE)CI=xˉ±(tc​×SE)

Subpart Evaluations​
(i) 90% Confidence Interval​
  • Critical Value (tct_ctc​ for df=14df = 14df=14): 1.7613\mathbf{1.7613}1.7613
  • Margin of Error (MEMEME): 1.7613×2.5820=4.54771.7613 \times 2.5820 = \mathbf{4.5477}1.7613×2.5820=4.5477
  • Lower Limit: 78−4.5477=73.452378 - 4.5477 = \mathbf{73.4523}78−4.5477=73.4523
  • Upper Limit: 78+4.5477=82.552378 + 4.5477 = \mathbf{82.5523}78+4.5477=82.5523
  • Interval: [73.4523, 82.5523]≈[73.45, 82.55]\mathbf{[73.4523, \, 82.5523]} \approx \mathbf{[73.45, \, 82.55]}[73.4523,82.5523]≈[73.45,82.55]

  • Lower Limit: 78−5.5378=72.462278 - 5.5378 = \mathbf{72.4622}78−5.5378=72.4622

  • Interval: [72.4622, 83.5378]≈[72.46, 83.54]\mathbf{[72.4622, \, 83.5378]} \approx \mathbf{[72.46, \, 83.54]}[72.4622,83.5378]≈[72.46,83.54]

2. Practical Uses of the Normal Distribution (8 Marks)​

  1. Foundation of Parametric Statistical Inference:
    • Forms the mathematical backbone of classical hypothesis testing (ZZZ-tests, ttt-tests, ANOVA FFF-tests, Chi-square tests), all of which assume underlying or asymptotic normality.
  2. Central Limit Theorem (CLT) Vehicle:
    • Under CLT, sample means from any finite-variance distribution converge to a normal distribution as sample size grows (n≥30n \ge 30n≥30). This enables inference on complex population parameters without knowing the parent distribution.
  3. Statistical Quality Control & Six Sigma:
    • Industrial manufacturing uses Gaussian control charts (Xˉ\bar{X}Xˉ and RRR charts) with action limits set at μ±3σ\mu \pm 3\sigmaμ±3σ (99.73%99.73\%99.73% tolerance band) to detect process anomalies.
  4. Grading on a Curve and Psychometrics:
    • Standardizes educational assessments and IQ tests (mean 100, SD 15) to ensure grading criteria remain invariant across different examination cohorts.
  5. Risk Management & Quantitative Finance:
    • Models asset log-returns and calculates Value-at-Risk (VaR) thresholds in investment portfolios.

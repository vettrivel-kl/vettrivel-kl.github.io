Question 1: Travelling Salesman Problem (Search Formulation & Heuristics)​

info

Question Statement

  • Does Alpha-Beta ever alter the root value? NO.

3. Chess Time Limit & Alpha-Beta Move Ordering​

  1. Anytime Property under Time Constraints:
    A fixed-depth search cannot predict whether depth ddd will finish within 10 seconds; if interrupted, it returns no move. IDS is an anytime algorithm: if the timer runs out during iteration ddd, the engine immediately outputs the best move completed at iteration d−1d-1d−1.
  2. Move Ordering for Alpha-Beta Pruning:
    Alpha-Beta pruning reaches optimal O(bd/2)O(b^{d/2})O(bd/2) efficiency only if best moves are evaluated first. The best move identified at depth d−1d-1d−1 (the principal variation) is tried first at depth ddd, maximizing beta-cutoffs and pruning large swaths of the game tree.
